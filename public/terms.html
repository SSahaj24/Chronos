Chronos terms

Chronos is a free personal project, provided as is, with no guarantee that it will stay available or be free of bugs.

It only reads the Notion pages you share with it, and only writes to Notion when you ask it to create a Journal. You can disconnect it from Notion at any time.

Don't use it for anything you'd be upset to lose. Your data always stays in your own Notion.
