Chronos privacy

Chronos is a small personal project that draws a time dashboard from a Notion Journal.

What we store

  • Your Notion name, email, avatar and workspace name, from Notion's sign-in.
  • Your Notion access token, encrypted. It's used only to read the pages you chose to share, and to create a Journal if you ask for one.
  • Which database is your Journal, and your class colour choices.
  • A session cookie to keep you signed in for 30 days.

What we don't store

Your Journal entries are read from Notion each time you open the dashboard and are not saved on our side. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, and there's no tracking or analytics.

Removing your data

Use "Delete my account" at the bottom of the dashboard to remove everything stored about you. You can also disconnect the app in Notion under Settings → Connections, which revokes its access immediately.
